The amount that you will be paid on your notes at maturity, if they have not been automatically called, in addition to the final coupon, if any, is based on the index stock return. The index stock return is the percentage increase or decrease in the closing price of the index stock on the determination date (the final observation date, June 1, 2027) from the initial index stock price.

if the index stock return is less than -38% (the final index stock price is less than 62% of the initial index stock price), the sum of (i) $1,000 plus (ii) the product of (a) the index stock return times (b) $1,000. You will receive less than 62% of the face amount of your notes and no coupon.
