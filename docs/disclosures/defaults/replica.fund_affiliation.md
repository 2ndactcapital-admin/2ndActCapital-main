# Fund replica — no affiliation

*Default — have counsel review. `{ticker}` is replaced by the fund's ticker wherever this is shown.*

A replica model is built from the fund's **published** holdings, which are reported with a delay. The platform and
the firm are not affiliated with the fund's sponsor or its index provider, and neither has reviewed or endorsed the
replica.

- The replica holds individual securities in your account, not shares of the fund.
- Its holdings and weights will drift from the fund's between rebalances, and differ because of minimum trade
  sizes, exclusions, fractional-share limits and tax management.
- Its costs (trading, the firm's fee) differ from the fund's expense ratio.
- Its results will differ from the fund's. Read the fund's prospectus for the fund itself.
