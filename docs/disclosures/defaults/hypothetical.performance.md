# Hypothetical and backtested performance

*Default — have counsel review. The firm's approved hypothetical-performance policy (SEC Marketing Rule,
Rule 206(4)-1) decides who may see hypothetical performance at all. This text is a starting point for the
firm's own disclosure; a firm's approved policy text is never replaced by this default.*

## What these results are

- **Backtested.** The figures show how a model would have performed if it had been applied to past market data.
  No account was managed this way during the period shown.
- **Prepared with hindsight.** The model and its rules were chosen knowing how markets behaved, which tends to make
  results look better than real-time results would have been.
- **Not actual trading.** They do not reflect trading costs, market impact, taxes or decisions an adviser would
  actually have made, unless stated.
- **Assumptions.** Prices, dividends and harvested losses are estimated from data that may be incomplete. Where a
  security had no price for a day, it was treated as cash.

## Limits

Past performance — actual or hypothetical — does not guarantee future results. Any investment can lose money.
Hypothetical performance is shown only to the people the firm's policy allows, together with this disclosure.
