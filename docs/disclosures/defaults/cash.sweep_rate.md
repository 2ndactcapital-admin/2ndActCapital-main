# Cash sweep rate

*Default — have counsel review. Written by the platform as a starting point.*

Uninvested cash in your account may be swept by the custodian into a bank deposit or money-market program. The rate
that program pays, and any spread or compensation the custodian or the firm receives on it, are disclosed in our
Form ADV (Part 2A, Item 5). The rate can change at any time and may differ from money-market fund yields.
