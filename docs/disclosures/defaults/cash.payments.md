# Payments between your accounts

*Default — have counsel review. Written by the platform as a starting point.*

## 1. What a payment instruction is

A payment instruction is a request, approved by two different people at the firm, that your custodian move a stated
amount from one of your accounts to another account with the same owner, by a stated method (ACH, wire, a journal
between accounts at the same custodian, or a check), on a business day. The firm prepares and sends the instruction;
the custodian moves the money and confirms it.

## 2. Settlement and returns

- **ACH** settles provisionally. For a period after settlement the receiving or sending bank can return the transfer
  (for example for insufficient funds, a closed account or an unauthorised debit). A returned transfer is reversed,
  and the firm records the return code it receives.
- **Wire** transfers are final once the custodian sends them.
- **Journals** between accounts at the same custodian settle when the custodian posts them.
- **Checks** settle when the check clears.

## 3. Dates

A value date is a U.S. business day. A request for a weekend or a federal holiday is not accepted; choose the next
business day. Cut-off times at the custodian may move a same-day request to the next business day.

## 4. Who can ask for one

Only accounts the firm manages can be instructed, and only by people the firm has authorised for that account's owner.
Payments to anyone other than the owner (third parties) are not offered.

## 5. Records

Every instruction, approval, confirmation, return and reconciliation is recorded. Account numbers are shown by their
last four digits only.
