# Tax estimates

*Default — have counsel review.*

Every tax figure marked **estimate** is calculated from the tax profile (rates) recorded for the household and
from the cost-basis data the platform holds. It is not a tax return calculation and it is not tax advice.

- Rates come from the adviser with a date; nothing is assumed. With no approved tax profile, no tax value is shown.
- Cost basis and holding periods come from the custodian or from entered data and may be incomplete.
- Harvested losses usually **defer** tax rather than remove it, and the benefit depends on your future gains,
  income and tax rates.
- Tax law can change.

Confirm any tax figure with your tax adviser before acting on it.
