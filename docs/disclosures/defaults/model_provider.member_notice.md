# Model provided by a sub-adviser

*Default — have counsel review. Written by the platform as a starting point; it is not legal advice.*

## 1. What it means

Some models your adviser can use are provided by a sub-adviser: a registered investment adviser that builds a model
portfolio (its target weights) and maintains it over time. {provider} is the sub-adviser for this model.

## 2. Who is responsible for what

* **The sub-adviser** decides the model's target weights and publishes each new version.
* **Your adviser** decides whether the model suits your account, whether to adopt each new version, and every trade
  in your account, including tax-aware adjustments such as harvesting losses or deferring gains. A new version never
  trades on its own.
* **The sub-adviser never receives** your name, your household, your account or your positions. It receives only the
  total assets managed to its model across all of your adviser's clients, which it needs to calculate its fee.

## 3. Fees

The sub-adviser is paid a model-provider fee, an annual rate in basis points of the assets managed to its model,
accrued daily. Ask your adviser whether this fee is passed through to you or paid by the adviser.

## 4. When a subscription ends

If your adviser's subscription to the model is suspended or ends, your account keeps the version it follows until
your adviser moves it to another model.

## 5. More information

The sub-adviser's Form ADV Part 2A describes its business, conflicts of interest and fees. Your adviser's own Form ADV
describes its use of sub-advisers.
