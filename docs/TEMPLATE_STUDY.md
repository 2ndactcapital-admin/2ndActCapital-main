## Misleading-label flags

Every item the model flagged as misleading about what it really does, with its quote.

- **Threshold Value:** (Jefferies, 0001140361-26-002924) — Label 'Threshold Value' is presented as absolute index levels but functionally is the downside barrier (75%)  
  > Threshold Value: 19,517.09 with respect to the NDX (75% of its Initial Value); and 1,990.160 with respect to the RTY (75% of its Initial Value, rounded to three decimal places).
- **Coupon Barrier (5-year table)** (Goldman Sachs, 0000950103-26-005372) — The table shows a 'Trigger Event Observation Frequency' of 70% and separate 'Coupon Barrier' of 50% — the header placement may confuse which value is the coupon barrier.  
  > Trigger Event Observation Frequency 70% 70% 70% 70% Coupon Barrier 50% 50% 50% 50%
- **Total Discounts and Commissions (aggregate)** (Deutsche Bank, 0000950103-25-000655) — Table shows aggregate dollar amount of discounts/commissions but total_commissions_fees_pct expects a percent; mapping to percent would require computation relative to total offering, which is not explicitly given as percentage in the table.  
  > Total $19,674,790.00 $491,869.75 $19,182,920.25
- **Initial Value (NDX)** (TD, 0001140361-22-032181) — Label 'Initial Value' is an index level, not a date; maps_to initial_valuation_date is not exact but this item is the numeric initial index level  
  > With respect to NDX: 12,274.63
- **Stated principal amount:** (UBS, 0000914121-19-003357) — Called 'stated principal amount' but repayment of that principal is NOT guaranteed and depends on the underlying in many scenarios.  
  > Stated principal amount: $10.00 per security
- **Per security: Proceeds to issuer:** (Citigroup, 0000950103-19-001014) — Table layout is ambiguous; $9.75 is labeled as proceeds but table columns are unclear without footnote interpretation.  
  > Proceeds to issuer Per security: $10.00 $0.20 (2) $9.75 $0.05 (3)
- **(1) selling concession cap** (Royal Bank of Canada, 0000950103-26-012495) — Label suggests a single 'agent commission %' but text gives a per-$1,000 maximum selling concession (monetary), not a single stated agent percentage.  
  > we or one of our affiliates may pay varying selling concessions of up to $25.00 per $1,000 principal amount of Notes
- **Discounts and Commissions (Per Security)** (Deutsche Bank, 0000950103-25-000655) — Shown as 'Discounts and Commissions' per security; this is the agent discount amount per $10 face ($0.25), which equals 2.5% of face, not necessarily the full set of fees disclosed elsewhere.  
  > Per Security $10.00 $0.25 $9.75
- **Original Offering Price:** (Wells Fargo, 0001387131-20-005470) — Although stated as $1,000 per note, the original offering price can vary for certain investors between $993.50 and $1,000.  
  > Original Offering Price: $1,000 per note; provided that the original offering price for an eligible institutional investor and an investor purchasing the notes in a fee-based advisory account will vary but will not be less than $993.50 per note and will not be more than $1,000 per note.
- **Trigger Event Observation Frequency (3-year table)** (Goldman Sachs, 0000950103-26-005372) — Label 'Trigger Event Observation Frequency' appears as a percent in the table (e.g., 60%) but likely represents the observation frequency/threshold rather than a simple percentage frequency.  
  > Trigger Event Observation Frequency 60% 60% 60% 60%
